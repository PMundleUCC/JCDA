Our story · Established 2012

Change begins when neighbours work together.

JCDA began in 2012 as a small group of residents concerned about youth opportunities, neglected public spaces and the need for stronger communication across Kingston communities.

What started with neighbourhood meetings and weekend clean-up activities grew into an organized association supported by volunteers, local businesses and public-sector partners.

Today, JCDA continues to listen, organize and act. Our programmes focus on community improvement, youth development, environmental care and support for families who need it most.

Our journey

Milestones that shaped JCDA

Each step reflects the time, ideas and energy contributed by our community.

  1. 2012

    JCDA is formed

    Residents establish a community association and begin monthly neighbourhood meetings.

  2. 2016

    Youth programme launches

    Our first skills and mentorship programme welcomes 140 young people from across all fourteen Parish.

  3. 2020

    Family support expands

    JCDA coordinates food and care packages for more than 200 households during a period of need.

  4. 2025

    Community network grows

    New partnerships help us deliver environmental, education and public-space projects.
